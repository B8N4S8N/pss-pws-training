# OHA Approval Playbook — Cascade Peer Academy

**Goal:** Become an OHA-approved Traditional Health Worker (THW) training program for Peer Support Specialist (PSS, 40h) and Peer Wellness Specialist (PWS, 80h), then scale the platform nationally.

**Primary sources**

---

## Critical truth

**Students cannot use your certificate toward Oregon THW certification until OHA approves your organization as a training program.**

The software, curriculum, and AI labs are necessary — not sufficient. Approval is an organizational + instructional + assessment decision by the TEMPS subcommittee.

There is no “sure-fire” guarantee. What follows is the strongest practical path to maximize approval odds.

---

## Part A — What OHA will judge you on

### 1. You are a real organization
You need a legal entity that can:
- Contract, insure, and hold student records
- Name responsible parties and instructors
- Issue certificates of completion under the org name
- Submit quarterly workforce reports after approval

Common forms: nonprofit, behavioral health provider, recovery center, college, LLC/educational company.

### 2. Instructors are credible peers + educators
OHA evaluates instructor qualifications (not a single “must have Master’s” rule). Strong packages include:
- Certified PSS and/or PWS (or equivalent)
- Years of peer practice
- Facilitation / adult education experience
- Trauma-informed, ethics, MI / recovery coaching training
- Supervisor experience preferred
- **Experienced THWs involved in developing and teaching the core curriculum** (OAR requirement)

Weak: recently certified person with no teaching history.  
Strong: decade of peer work + facilitation + trauma/MI credentials.

### 3. Curriculum maps to OAR 950-060-0140
| Credential | Hours | Must cover |
|------------|-------|------------|
| PSS | ≥40 contact hours | Core topics including (2)(a)–(p) + peer role/scope + recovery/resilience/wellness |
| PWS | ≥80 contact hours | Full core (2)(a)–(bb) + PWS extras (self-efficacy, group facilitation, resilience, recovery/wellness models, MI) |

Platform curriculum in `src/lib/curriculum/courses.ts` is built to this map — still must be packaged as syllabus, instructor guide, student handbook, and assessments for TEMPS.

### 4. Assessment is multi-modal and human-validated
OAR expects written, oral, and/or practical evaluation of knowledge and skill mastery.

**Winning frame for Cascade:**
> AI is a high-repetition practice environment. Human instructors observe roleplay, review portfolios, and authorize certificates of completion.

**Losing frame:**
> AI replaces instructors / AI alone certifies students.

### 5. Online / hybrid is allowed if competency is proven — but pure AYOP is a rejection risk
OHA’s official application FAQ states clearly:

> **“At this time, self-paced online trainings will not be approved.”** Exceptions are considered individually and must prove engagement, competency demonstration, graduation requirements, and workforce readiness.

**Implication for Cascade:** Do **not** pitch “100% self-paced AYOP” to TEMPS. Pitch:
- Self-paced knowledge modules **plus**
- Required live virtual workshops / observed roleplays **plus**
- Final human competency evaluation

Market “flexible online” to students; submit “hybrid competency-based” to OHA.

### 6. One application per worker type
FAQ: if offering PSS **and** PWS, submit **separate applications and curricula** for each.

### 7. Non-CBO organizations need a CBO partner letter
If you are not a Community-Based Organization under OAR 950-060, you need a signed agreement / letter of support / MOU with a CBO.

### 8. Records & certificate language (FAQ-mandated fields)
Certificate of completion must include:
- Participant name, date range, worker type/subtype, hours
- Trainer signature (electronic OK)
- Organization name + insignia (and partner orgs if any)
- **OARs associated with Traditional Health Workers**
- **“With lived experience”** for PSS/PWS
- Whether oral health training was included

Also maintain attendance, quizzes, evaluations, instructor roster, training dates, student feedback form, grievance/attendance policies, and a separate certificate of attendance/participation when full completion isn’t met.

### 9. After approval: quarterly reports
Approved programs submit the THW Training Quarterly Report.

**OHA contacts:** submit to THW.Program@odhsoha.oregon.gov · application help (FAQ): Shaun Cook — shaun.f.cook@oha.oregon.gov

After your training certificate, students still must:
1. Complete OHA-approved **oral health** training  
2. Pass background check (as applicable)  
3. Apply via Health Workforce Registry / THW process  
4. For **CRM (MHACBO)**: use an OHA-approved **addiction** peer training + 2 years recovery + MHACBO process ([MHACBO CRM](https://www.mhacbo.org/en/peers/crm/)) — CRM ≠ OHA PSS registry path

## Part C — What “sure-fire” actually means (risk controls)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| TEMPS rejects “AI certification” | Explicit human final competency evaluation; AI = practice only |
| Incomplete packet | Self-score rubric to 3–4 on every item; labeled attachments |
| Weak instructors | Hire/partner experienced certified peers with facilitation proof |
| Hours don’t add up | Publish contact-hour matrix summing to 40 / 80 |
| Online format skepticism | Hybrid workshops + required live observed roleplays for all tracks |
| Missing lived-experience language | Completion certificate + attestation workflow |
| Slow OHA cycle | Target Nov 2026 slot; submit by Sept 6, 2026; start packet now |
| Scope creep (building features vs approval) | Freeze OHA packet content; ship platform polish in parallel |

## Part D — National expansion: your “4-inch lap” strategy

Your roofing analogy is right for **skills content** and wrong if it means **one certificate replaces every state board**.

### How U.S. peer certification actually works
- ~49 states have peer credentials; requirements vary widely  
  ([BHWRC analysis](https://www.healthworkforceta.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/BHWRC_SOPs.pdf): median ~46 training hours; practice hours 0–2000)
- SAMHSA model standards suggest **40–60 training hours** as a national reference ([PEP23-10-01-001](https://library.samhsa.gov/sites/default/files/pep23-10-01-001.pdf))
- **Each state (or board) still approves training and issues/recognizes the credential**
- Reciprocity is limited (Oregon THW reciprocity currently narrow — e.g., AK/ID pathways for some types)

### Recommended product architecture (platform for every state)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  UNIVERSAL CORE (the “4-inch lap”)          │
│  SAMHSA core competencies + strictest       │
│  common topics: ethics, trauma, MI, crisis, │
│  boundaries, documentation, cultural        │
│  humility, recovery principles, etc.        │
│  Delivered as competency modules (not       │
│  “state hours” alone)                       │
└─────────────────┬───────────────────────────┘
                  │
      ┌───────────┼───────────┐
      ▼           ▼           ▼
┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐
│ Oregon   │ │ State B  │ │ State C  │
│ Overlay  │ │ Overlay  │ │ Overlay  │
│ 40/80h   │ │ hours    │ │ hours    │
│ OAR map  │ │ board    │ │ exam     │
│ PSS/PWS  │ │ unique   │ │ practice │
│ + CRM    │ │ modules  │ │ hrs      │
│ path     │ │          │ │          │
└──────────┘ └──────────┘ └──────────┘
```

**Choose-your-state UX:** learner picks state → sees the exact credential pathway(s) → completes Core + that state’s Overlay → receives state-specific certificate of completion recognized by that state’s approval.

### Can one course cover all states?
**Partially yes for curriculum depth; no for automatic legal certification.**

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **A. Superset single course** (always do the “4-inch lap”) | One teaching method; high competence; simpler ops | May exceed some states’ hour minima (cost/time); still need each state to accept the program; unique state exams/practice hours remain |
| **B. Core + state overlays** (recommended) | Honest compliance; scalable; clear certificates per jurisdiction | More product complexity |
| **C. Separate course per state from scratch** | Simple legally | Unmaintainable at 50 states |

**Practical hybrid (best):**
1. Build **Universal Core** to the strictest common content bar (ethics depth, crisis, MI, trauma, cultural humility, documentation, harm reduction, etc.)
2. Add **State Overlay** for hours math, unique required topics, assessment quirks, certificate language
3. Where a state’s minimum is 40h and your Core is 60h, the Overlay may be thin — student still finishes a stronger program (your “always 4 inches”)
4. Never claim “one certificate = certified in all 50 states”

### Oregon → national sequencing
1. **Oregon OHA approval** (PSS + PWS) — prove TEMPS + digital model  
2. **Oregon CRM adjacency** — ensure addiction specialization pathway accepted for MHACBO CRM applicants  
3. **Neighbor states** (WA, ID, CA, AK) — reciprocity/workforce corridors  
4. **High-volume Medicaid peer states** — expand overlays  
5. **National Core marketed to employers** even before every state approval (workforce readiness + CE), while state-approved tracks remain the certification product

### Platform features to build for multi-state (later)
- State selector + credential catalog
- Competency graph shared across states
- Overlay hour calculator + gap modules
- Per-state certificate templates + board submission checklists
- Admin: multi-jurisdiction program approvals, instructor eligibility by state
- Employer network filtered by state Medicaid peer billing rules
