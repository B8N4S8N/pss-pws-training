# Layman’s guide — Cascade Peer Academy

## What is this?

An online school that trains people to become **Peer Support Specialists** (40-hour class) or **Peer Wellness Specialists** (80-hour class) in Oregon.

Peers are people with **lived experience** of mental health, addiction, or family experience who help others as part of the care team — not as doctors or therapists.

## Why Oregon cares about “OHA approval”

In Oregon, your training program must be approved by the **Oregon Health Authority** (Traditional Health Worker program). After you finish an approved class, you get a **certificate of completion**. Then you still apply to OHA (plus oral health training and background check steps) to become state-certified.

This platform is built so the organization can show OHA: curriculum, tests, practice, attendance, and final instructor sign-off.

## How students learn

1. Read short lessons and take quizzes  
2. Practice “what would you say?” scenarios  
3. Talk with **AI practice clients** (pretend peers in hard situations) and get feedback  
4. Use the **AI tutor** when stuck  
5. Practice a **mock job interview**  
6. If hybrid: join live Zoom workshops  
7. Finish with a **live evaluation with a real instructor**

## Student dashboard

After login you see your course progress, hours, upcoming live sessions (if hybrid), certificates, and links to Practice Lab / Tutor / Interview.

## Admin dashboard (teachers)

Staff can see every student, quiz history, AI practice scores, mark attendance, write competency evaluations, and issue certificates.

## Delivery options

- **AYOP:** learn mostly on your own schedule; still do a live final check-in  
- **Hybrid:** online lessons + scheduled live classes
